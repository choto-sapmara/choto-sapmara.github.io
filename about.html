History

Choto Sapmara Government Primary School has been a cornerstone of education in the community for many years. Established with the mission to provide quality primary education to the children of Choto Sapmara and surrounding areas, the school has grown from humble beginnings into a respected institution.

The school is located in a peaceful rural setting, providing an ideal environment for young learners. Over the years, the school has produced many successful students who have gone on to excel in their higher studies and careers.

Our Vision

To be a leading primary educational institution that empowers students with knowledge, skills, and values to become responsible global citizens while preserving our cultural heritage.

Our Mission

To provide holistic primary education that fosters academic excellence, critical thinking, and character development in a supportive and nurturing learning environment. We strive to create lifelong learners who are confident, compassionate, and contributing members of society.

School Values

  • Excellence - Striving for the highest standards in education
  • Integrity - Building character through honesty and responsibility
  • Respect - Fostering mutual respect among students, teachers, and the community
  • Innovation - Embracing creative and modern teaching methods
  • Community - Working together for the betterment of all
