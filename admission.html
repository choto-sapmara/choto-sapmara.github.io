Admission Notice

Admission for the 2026-2027 academic year is now open. We welcome applications from children aged 5-6 years for Class 1. Limited seats are also available in higher classes subject to vacancy.

Eligibility

  • Children must be at least 5 years old by December 31 of the admission year.
  • Birth certificate issued by the local municipality is required.
  • Previous academic records for transfer students.
  • Immunization card must be submitted at the time of admission.

Required Documents

  • Birth certificate (original and photocopy)
  • Parent's/Guardian's NID card
  • 2 copies of passport-size photographs
  • Immunization card
  • Transfer certificate (for students from other schools)

Admission Procedure

Interested parents and guardians are requested to collect the admission form from the school office during working hours. Completed forms along with required documents should be submitted to the Head Teacher's office. Admission will be confirmed after verification of documents and a brief interaction with the child.

Contact for Admission

For any admission-related queries, please contact the school office at +8801673147369 or visit the school during working hours — Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
