Admission Notice

Admisson for this school is open from January to February of every year. We welcome children aged 5 to 6 years for Pre-Primary. Other children can admit to classes 1 to 5.

Eligibility

  • Children must be at least 5 years old by January 1 of the admission year.
  • Birth certificate issued by the local municipality is required.
  • Previous academic records for transfer students.

Required Documents

  • Birth certificate (original and photocopy)
  • Parent's/Guardian's NID card (photocopy)
  • 2 copies of passport-size photographs
  • Transfer certificate (for students from other schools)

Admission Procedure

Interested parents and guardians are requested to come to the office room during working hours from January to February of every year. Required documents should be submitted to the Head Teacher's office. Admission will be confirmed after verification of documents and a brief interaction with the child.

Contact for Admission
