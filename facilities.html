Classrooms

Our school has spacious, well-ventilated classrooms equipped with modern furniture and teaching aids to ensure a comfortable learning environment for all students.

📚

Library

The school library houses a rich collection of books, educational magazines, and reference materials to encourage reading habits among students.

Playground

A large playground provides ample space for outdoor sports and recreational activities essential for the physical development of students.

💻

Computer Lab

Our computer lab is equipped with computers to introduce students to the world of technology and digital literacy from an early age.

🏥

Health & Hygiene

The school maintains proper sanitation facilities and clean drinking water. Regular health check-ups are conducted for all students.

🎭

Auditorium

A multipurpose hall for school assemblies, cultural events, and co-curricular activities that help develop students' creativity and confidence.
