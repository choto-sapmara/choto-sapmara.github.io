A Message from the Head Teacher

Welcome to 57 No. Choto Sapmara Government Primary School. In these halls, we nurture not just academic growth, but the whole child — their curiosity, their character, and their dreams. Our dedicated faculty walks alongside each student, ensuring that every child who passes through our gates leaves with a stronger mind, a kinder heart, and the confidence to shape their own future. We invite you to be part of this journey with us.
