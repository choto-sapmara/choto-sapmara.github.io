A Place to Grow

At 57 No. Choto Sapmara Government Primary School, we believe in the holistic development of every student. Our school provides a nurturing environment where children can grow academically, socially, and morally. We encourage students to participate in a wide range of activities beyond the classroom — discovering talents, building friendships, and developing confidence that will last a lifetime.

Class Structure

Class 1 Foundation stage — basic literacy and numeracy
Class 2 Building reading and writing skills
Class 3 Introduction to science and social studies
Class 4 Advanced subjects and critical thinking
Class 5 Preparation for the PSC examination

Co-Curricular Activities

Sports

Football, cricket, athletics, and traditional games

Cultural Programs

Music, dance, drama, and recitation competitions

Debate & Quiz

Intellectual competitions to sharpen young minds

Art & Craft

Drawing, painting, and creative crafts

Scouting

Bangladesh Scout group for character building

Study Tours

Educational field trips to places of interest

Student Achievements

Our students have consistently performed well in PSC examinations and various upazila-level competitions. Many of our alumni have gone on to excel in higher secondary and university education. We take pride in every student's success and strive to bring out the best in each child.
