Welcome from the Head Teacher

At 57 No. Choto Sapmara Government Primary School, we believe every child carries within them a unique potential waiting to be discovered. Our role is to provide the environment, encouragement, and education that allows that potential to flourish. With a committed team of teachers and a supportive community, we work each day to build not just academic ability, but confidence, kindness, and curiosity in every student who walks through our doors.
