A Personal Note

Welcome to our school.

There is a particular kind of magic in a primary school — the first time a child reads a sentence on their own, the moment a difficult sum suddenly makes sense, the quiet pride of a hand raised in class. At 57 No. Choto Sapmara Government Primary School, we have the privilege of witnessing these moments every day. Our work is to protect them, to nurture them, and to make sure that every child who enters our gates leaves with more than they arrived with. Thank you for entrusting us with your children.
