A Word from Our Head Teacher

Dear Students, Parents, and Friends,

It is my privilege to welcome you to 57 No. Choto Sapmara Government Primary School, a place where the journey of learning begins with curiosity and wonder. For many of our students, these will be the most formative years of their lives, and we do not take that responsibility lightly.

Our promise is simple: every child who enters our school will be known, will be challenged, and will be cared for. We are deeply grateful to the families and community who entrust us with this most important work.

— Mohi Uddin Bhuiyan
